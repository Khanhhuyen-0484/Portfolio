Case study · Mobile App

Wander Journal

A travel journal concept for saving memories, trip notes and favorite locations in one personal space.

Focus

Memory keeping

Platform

iOS / Android

Goal

Personal journaling

Problem

Travel memories are scattered.

Notes, photos and places often end up across different apps, making it hard to revisit a trip later.

  • Capture stories quickly after a trip.
  • Keep places, photos and notes together.
  • Make journaling feel effortless.

Solution

Trip-based journaling

  • Trip timelines and location pins.
  • Quick photo and note saving.
  • Clean, calming mobile interface.
