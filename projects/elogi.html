Case study · Website

ELOGI

Creating a premium fashion website concept that balances editorial storytelling with a clean shopping flow.

Focus

Luxury browsing experience

Platform

Responsive web

Outcome

Stronger visual storytelling

The problem

Premium fashion needs a more refined digital presence.

Many fashion websites overload users with products, promotions and weak hierarchy, making the brand feel less premium and harder to explore.

  • The visual tone needed to feel elegant and editorial.
  • Navigation had to guide users without overwhelming them.
  • The layout needed to support both discovery and conversion.

Goals

Design principles

  • Highlight products with strong imagery and whitespace.
  • Keep shopping actions easy to find and understand.
  • Balance branding with usability on all screen sizes.
  • Create a polished first impression for premium audiences.

Experience

Editorial and conversion-friendly.

The concept combines a visual-first homepage, curated collections and clear product pathways so users can browse with confidence.

  • High-impact hero sections for seasonal storytelling.
  • Collection blocks with a consistent grid system.
  • Clear CTAs to move from inspiration to purchase.

Visual direction

Minimal, modern and premium.

The design uses generous spacing, restrained typography and high-quality imagery to give the brand a refined voice.

  • Neutral palette with subtle contrast.
  • Elegant typography pairing for fashion storytelling.
  • Responsive layouts for a smooth shopping experience on desktop and mobile.
