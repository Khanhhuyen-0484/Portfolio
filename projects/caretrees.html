Care Trees App

Thiết kế trải nghiệm mobile thân thiện giúp người trồng cây theo dõi tưới nước, ánh sáng và các việc chăm sóc.

Trọng tâm

Habit-forming reminders

Nền tảng

iOS / Android

Kết quả

Simple daily engagement

The problem

Plant care is easy to forget.

Many plant owners struggle to remember watering schedules and care routines, especially when they own multiple plants with different needs.

  • Users needed quick reminders instead of complex task management.
  • Care instructions had to be easy to understand at a glance.
  • The experience needed to feel calm, friendly and motivating.

Mục tiêus

Design principles

  • Make plant care feel effortless and rewarding.
  • Support daily routines with lightweight notifications.
  • Keep navigation simple for fast access on mobile.
  • Use a warm visual style that feels organic and approachable.

Features

Helpful and simple actions.

The concept centers on a plant list, care reminders, and progress feedback that encourages users to stay consistent.

  • Personalized watering and sunlight reminders.
  • Plant detail cards with care tips and status.
  • Quick actions for marking tasks as complete.

Visual direction

Soft, fresh and mobile-first.

The UI uses bright natural tones, rounded cards and clear hierarchy to create a welcoming feel while keeping the interface practical.

  • Rounded containers and spacious layout for easy scanning.
  • Friendly illustrations and subtle motion cues.
  • Strong contrast for accessible reading on small screens.
