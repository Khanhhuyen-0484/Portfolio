Atlas Design System

A reusable component library concept designed to help teams ship consistent interfaces faster.

Focus

Consistency

Platform

Product teams

Outcome

Problem

Teams need consistency at scale.

As products grow, UI inconsistency slows teams down and makes collaboration harder.

  • Standardize buttons, cards and forms.
  • Reduce design and dev duplication.
  • Make the system easy to adopt.

Solution

Reusable foundation

  • Core tokens and component rules.
  • Flexible variants and states.
  • Clear documentation structure.
