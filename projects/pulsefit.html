PulseFit

A mobile wellness concept for tracking workouts, habits and daily motivation with a calm, energetic interface.

Trọng tâm

Habit building

Nền tảng

iOS / Android

Mục tiêu

Daily retention

Vấn đề

Fitness apps often feel overwhelming.

Users wanted a simple place to track movement, habits and progress without getting lost in charts and crowded dashboards.

  • Fast access to today’s workout and streak status.
  • Clear progress feedback without clutter.
  • Motivation that feels supportive, not pushy.

Giải pháp

Lightweight daily flow

  • One-screen daily summary.
  • Quick habit check-ins and streaks.
  • Friendly visual cues for progress.

Muốn xây dựng một app wellness?

Mình thiết kế trải nghiệm mobile khuyến khích xây dựng thói quen và khiến hành động hằng ngày dễ duy trì hơn.
