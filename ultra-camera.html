Ultra Camera

A professional mobile camera experience for capturing, organizing and editing photos with a clean purple UI system.

Project overview

A complete camera flow from capture to final edit.

Ultra Camera is a mobile app concept designed for users who want a more capable camera experience on their phone. The app combines quick capture, gallery browsing and photo editing in one polished flow.

Scope

Capture, gallery and editing.

  • Three-step onboarding flow.
  • Camera home with quick feature entry points.
  • Camera screen with capture controls and quick tool actions.
  • Gallery layout for browsing saved photos.
  • Photo editor with blur, eraser and adjustment controls.

Problem

Advanced camera tools can feel too technical.

  • Users need fast access to capture, gallery and edit actions without searching through menus.
  • Editing controls need to feel powerful while staying clear and tap-friendly.
  • The interface needs to balance a professional feature set with a friendly visual tone.

Goal

Make pro-style camera actions easier to understand.

  • Create a clear first-run experience that explains the app quickly.
  • Highlight the main camera action while keeping secondary tools visible.
  • Design a gallery that feels visual, organized and easy to scan.
  • Make editing controls readable through strong spacing and hierarchy.

Visual design

Soft purple, clean cards and strong capture actions.

  • Purple is used as the primary brand color for buttons, active states and highlights.
  • Rounded cards make the feature sections feel friendly and modern.
  • The camera action is treated as the strongest visual element in the home flow.
  • Editing sliders and tool buttons are separated clearly to reduce visual clutter.

Reflection

Designing a camera app that feels capable but simple.

Ultra Camera helped me explore how to present advanced photo features in a clean mobile interface. The result is a focused flow that supports capture, browsing and editing without overwhelming the user.
