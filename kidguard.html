Case study - Family Safety App

KidGuard App

A parental control experience that helps families manage screen time, safe apps and child device access with calm, friendly UI.

View Screens Back to Projects

Project overview

A friendly control center for parent and child devices.

KidGuard is designed to help parents manage device usage while keeping the child experience simple and understandable. The app covers screen time, app limits, child setup, lock states and time schedules.

Scope

Screen time, setup and safe access.

  • Parent dashboard with usage history.
  • Daily and custom time setting flows.
  • Child app home and safe play states.
  • Child device setup and connection flow.
  • Lock screen state when time runs out.

Problem

Parental control apps can feel strict and stressful.

  • Parents need clear controls for screen time and app access.
  • Children need lock states that feel understandable, not confusing.
  • Setup must make device connection and permissions feel simple.

Goal

Make safety controls feel calm, visual and easy to adjust.

  • Use friendly illustrations and soft colors to reduce tension.
  • Surface the most important usage numbers clearly.
  • Separate parent controls from child-facing screens.
  • Make time limits and lock states easy to understand at a glance.

Visual design

Bright, safe and family-friendly.

  • Blue communicates structure and trust for parent controls.
  • Soft green and yellow accents make child-facing screens feel warmer.
  • Large cards and circular progress indicators make usage data easy to scan.
  • Friendly illustrations soften the lock and time-limit experience.
